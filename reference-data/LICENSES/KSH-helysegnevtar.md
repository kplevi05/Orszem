## Required attribution

Any distribution of data derived from this source must display:

> Forrás: KSH — https://www.ksh.hu

The KSH terms require that the attribution is not hidden or shown separately from the
data, and that an online citation is a working link.

## Scope note

KSH applies a different licence — CC BY-NC 4.0, excluding commercial use — to datasets
extracted from its internal databases **on individual request**. That does not apply to the
Helységnévtár, which is a standard published download rather than a custom extract.

If a future dataset is ever obtained by individual request, its licence must be
re-checked; it will not be CC BY 4.0.
